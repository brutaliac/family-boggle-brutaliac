    How to play

    Find words of 3 or more letters by linking neighboring letters, sideways, up and down, or diagonally. Each letter cube can be used once per word. The Qu cube counts as both letters. Everyone plays the same board at the same time, and words stay hidden until the time is up. A word found by more than one player scores for each of them.
